Method, and what this cannot tell you

How a score is built

  1. Each pillar is normalised across the candidates on screen, so every score is relative to this set and not an absolute rating. Cost and talent are log-scaled because both span orders of magnitude; cost is the only pillar where less is better.
  2. Pillars are combined at the weights on the left.
  3. The stability column re-runs the ranking 2,000 times, drawing weights around your current position and redrawing every capability share from the binomial that produced it. A candidate is robust only if it holds a top-three place in 90% of those runs and rests on at least postings.
